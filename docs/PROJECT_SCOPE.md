# Project Scope

The system simulates branch-based retail banking for four roles. In scope: customer and employee records, account opening/status, deposits, withdrawals, atomic internal transfers, ledger history, loan application/decision/disbursement/payment/history, role dashboards, reports, and audit history. Managers and employees perform financial work only for their assigned branch; the source account determines staff transfer scope. Customer registration is institution-wide, while later customer/account mutations are branch checked. Customers see and use only owned records. Out of scope: real money movement, payment gateways/cards, beneficiaries, external bank networks, SMS/OTP, biometrics, cryptocurrency, AI fraud scoring, and mobile apps. Financial records are never physically updated or deleted.
