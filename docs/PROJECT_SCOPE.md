# Project Scope

The system simulates branch-based retail banking for four roles. In scope: customer and employee records, account opening/status, deposits, withdrawals, atomic internal transfers, ledger history, loan application/decision/disbursement/payment, role dashboards, reports, and audit history. Out of scope: real money movement, payment gateways/cards, external bank networks, SMS/OTP, biometrics, cryptocurrency, AI fraud scoring, and mobile apps. Financial records are never physically deleted.
